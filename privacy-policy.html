Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire or place a booking with Swami Tours & Travels, we collect personal contact details including your full name, telephone / WhatsApp number, pickup and drop-off locations, date of travel, passenger count, and any specific route instructions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly utilized for:

  • Dispatching vehicle details, driver name, and vehicle registration numbers.
  • Coordinating pickup timing and communication between passenger and chauffeur.
  • Generating transparent travel trip receipts and invoices.
  • Customer service follow-up and emergency road assistance.

3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your telephone number or itinerary with any 3rd party advertising or marketing networks. Driver personnel only receive the passenger phone number necessary to coordinate timely pickup.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses basic performance cookies and local storage strictly to remember session preferences (such as selected route estimator parameters) and ensure fast page loading.

5. Contacting Our Data Officer

For any queries regarding personal data deletion or privacy concerns, please contact our support team at privacy@swamitoursandtravels.in or call +91 70838 08483.
