# Swami Tours & Travels — Strict UI Redesign Project

## Project Overview
- **Client**: Swami Tours & Travels (Guhagar / Ratnagiri / Mumbai / Pune)
- **Website**: https://swamitoursandtravels.in/
- **Objective**: Complete UI/UX redesign and visual elevation to a Luxury Navy Blue, Gold, and White theme, while strictly maintaining 100% of existing business data, rates, routes, car models, images, phone numbers, addresses, and technical SEO structure.

## Core Directives & Invariants
1. **Zero Content Mutation**: No added, deleted, modified, or rewritten business facts. No fake testimonials, no altered phone numbers (`+91 70838 08483`), no altered address (`Shringartali, Guhagar, Ratnagiri - 415721`), no altered vehicle names, rates, or specs.
2. **Visual Elevation**:
   - Primary: Deep Imperial Navy Blue (`#0A1128`, `#0F1A36`, `#162347`)
   - Accent: Warm Metallic Gold & Champagne (`#D4AF37`, `#F5D061`, `#E5B842`)
   - Surface / Balance: Crisp White & Porcelain Alabaster (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`)
   - Modern typography (`Outfit`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Noto Sans Devanagari`)
   - Custom SVG icons (zero emoji UI icons)
   - Executive automotive aesthetic (showroom podiums, clean badge metadata)
3. **Preserved Functionality**:
   - Fare Calculator with dynamic calculations & slabs
   - Quick Quote popups & PDF downloads
   - WhatsApp deep links with exact prefilled messages
   - Netlify booking form with honeypot & action routing
   - Testimonial rendering & local storage persistence
   - FAQ accordion & mobile responsive drawer
   - Developer credit modal (Rushikesh Online Services)
4. **Technical SEO Integrity**:
   - Preserve canonical tags, schema.org JSON-LD (TravelAgency, Product, FAQPage)
   - Ensure `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are aligned and accurate
   - Clean URLs and mobile responsive viewport compliance
